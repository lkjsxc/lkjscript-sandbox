# Native acceptance screenshots

These are actual Chromium captures of the writing and library implementation,
not design mockups. They use synthetic loopback accounts, text, and media; they
do not show public-service users or private operator data.

The original captures were taken on October 12, 2026 against the accepted
435-test candidate, application SHA-256
`ad675ced642e2d621dff7ff3b0500575af621c6877980080586777210a929393`.
The screenshots were recaptured after the native keyboard-submission fix.

[Desktop preview](preview-desktop.png) shows the saved owner-only review step.
[Mobile writing](writing-mobile.png) shows explicit save-before-media actions.
[Mobile selection in dark mode](selection-mobile-dark.png) shows the selected-file
tray independently of the current search results.

The screenshots are visual evidence, not an accessibility-conformance statement
or a claim that the public service is already running this candidate.
